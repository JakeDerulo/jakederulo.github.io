Ringflip Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Ringflip ("the app", "we") collects and how it is used. The app is published by Jaakko Heikkinen ("the developer").

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts.

The app saves your game progress, such as your coins, unlocked skins, scores, daily streak, missions and settings, in the storage of your own device. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted.

2. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads, including banner ads, full-screen ads between games, and optional rewarded video ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information from your device, such as your advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This may include using that information to show personalized ads. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or another region that requires it, the app shows a consent form before ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from the Settings screen in the app (Ad privacy options).
  • On iOS, the app asks for your permission before tracking, and you can change it in your device settings.
  • On Android and iOS you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device's privacy settings.

4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

5. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. The only third party that receives data through the app is the ad provider described above. We do not keep personal data about you, because we do not collect it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal data about you, most requests relate to Google's ad services, which you can manage through your Google account and device settings. You can also contact us with any question.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app works, for example by adding analytics or new services, we will update this page and the date at the top.
